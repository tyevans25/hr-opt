THE POSITION
The Data Scientist, Generosity Experimentation will play a pivotal role in growing an experimentation function within the Sportsbook Commercial team focused on retention and loyalty. Reporting to the Experimentation Manager, this role will help to accelerate the speed of learning while maintaining standardization of processes across teams. The Data Scientist will partner with retention marketing stakeholders and will be responsible for designing and analyzing experiments that help to answer key questions and drive promotional strategy. They will have the opportunity to work cross-functionally, aligning with commercial stakeholders, generosity data science, marketing technology and machine learning engineering to pursue a best-in-class personalization strategy.

THE GAME PLAN

- Compile results and present insights and recommendations to marketing leadership
- Continue the advancement of a cross-functional experimentation program
- Be a thought leader in identifying and evaluating tools and capabilities to advance our practice
- Introduce advanced statistical techniques to support data analysis and insight generation
- Drive culture of experimentation throughout the Sportsbook business
